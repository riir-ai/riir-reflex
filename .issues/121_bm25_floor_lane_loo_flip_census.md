## Why

The harness compares the modelless engine against NB, ridge, GLiNER, CLM, laya and hosted lanes — but has no STANDALONE lexical baseline: the NB/ridge scorers (`src/nb_ridge.rs`, `src/nb_scope.rs`) are the engine's own hashed-lexicon halves, not lanes, so nothing asserts the corpus-is-the-model engine beats plain keyword search over its own corpus. The paper makes that floor the admission test for any authored/dense memory (scoring under BM25-over-author-text = discarding the author's signal). And nothing measures per-row interference on the arena's corpora: which rows are dead weight (zero flips), which carry outsized blast radius (many flips), and whether the corpus sits in the paper's sparse band (≈ ≤2 flips/row) or the dense band (≈ 4.8+). The census doubles as the deletion cross-check for decision receipts, for free.

### A2 amendment — the small-n parity clause (2026-11-23, issue 126's delegated-owner verdict `060599b8`)

Strict `engine ≥ bm25` is decided by a handful of rows on a small suite: at n = 102 the paired 95% interval on (engine − bm25) spans about ±9 pp, so a one-row deficit is indistinguishable from a one-row surplus. The clause, recorded verbatim and ENFORCED BY CODE (`bm25_floor::floor_parity` — the verdict on every floor row is computed, never typed; Bench 205):

> A2 small-n parity clause: a suite with `engine < bm25` on a fresh two-run byte-identical record closes as **FLOOR-PARITY@n** (not PASS) iff ALL hold: (i) `n_eval < 400`; (ii) exact two-sided McNemar p ≥ 0.05, computed as the conditional binomial on the discordant pairs (b = bm25-right/engine-wrong, c = engine-right/bm25-wrong), p = min(1, 2·P(X ≤ min(b,c))), X ~ Bin(b+c, ½). If b+c = 0, then p = 1; (iii) point deficit `(b − c)/n ≤ 2.0 pp`. Otherwise the CORPUS-DEFECT finding stands. When n_eval ≥ 400, strict `engine ≥ bm25` applies unchanged.
> **Every floor row discloses:** n_eval, b, c, both-right, both-wrong, the exact p, the paired 95% CI on (engine − bm25) as Wald on the paired difference (`±1.96·√(b+c − (b−c)²/n)/n`), the point delta in rows and pp, and the verdict label (PASS / FLOOR-PARITY@n / CORPUS-DEFECT). PARITY rows also carry their re-open triggers.

Binding readings the verdict added: **FLOOR-PARITY retires the CORPUS-DEFECT finding and never counts as a PASS** for any promotion or GOAT claim; `p ≥ 0.05` alone is absence of evidence, hence the 2.0 pp deficit cap and the `n_eval < 400` restriction; and **the parity verdict expires on any change** to the fixtures, corpus or the engine's route/NB/ridge scoring (re-adjudicated on the next two-run sweep). A typed n/a row (issue 125) carries no parity field and no verdict.
