## TL;DR

The paper's headline result: when an LLM is asked for a 1–5 integer rating it regresses to the center and the response distribution collapses (KS similarity to human panels: GPT-4o 0.26, Gemini-2.0-flash 0.39); when it is instead asked for a short free-text statement whose embedding is compared by cosine similarity against five ordinal anchor statements — min-shifted, normalized, temperature-smeared into a pmf — it reaches 90% of human test-retest ranking reliability AND realistic distributions (KS > 0.85), zero-shot, no fine-tuning. **The mechanism class is published prior art** — cosine-to-label-description classification is the standard zero-shot embedding recipe (OpenAI cookbook 2022; Schopf et al. arXiv:2211.16285; the entailment lineage the paper itself cites, Yin et al. 2019) — so this is a **Gain, not a novelty claim**: the paper's durable gifts are (1) the empirical elicitation-shape law (numeric elicitation collapses distributions; the fix is elicitation shape, not model choice), (2) the pmf-construction details (min-shift against the narrow cosine band, ε smoothing, temperature, multi-anchor-set averaging), and (3) the **correlation-attainment metric** ρ = E[R_xy]/E[R_xx] — ranking fidelity normalized by a split-half test-retest noise ceiling. For the stack the fit is the **comparison-arena mission**: our hosted lanes return keyed-probs or exact-match-mapped text, and the harness reports accuracy/ECE only — there is no distributional-realism or ranking-signal axis. Issue 132 files the two actionable items: ordinal arena metrics (KS on ordinal suites — sst5 is the harness's only one, verified; total-variation separately labeled for unordered suites — and a within-suite per-case Spearman ranking axis, since the paper's between-concept attainment has NO valid adaptation here: one ordinal suite is one point, and the suite-prior control passes by construction under any between-suite design, §5b) and an opt-in anchor-similarity mapper for free-text hosted lanes that **degrades to abstain** instead of guessing (the SSR flat-pmf hazard is exactly a CorpusDistanceGate signal — the one fusion that makes this reflex-native). The modelless engine itself has no free text and no consumer here; the G10/G12 human-rater gates stay human (rejected, §6).

## 1. Paper Core Findings

Data: 57 personal-care concept surveys, 9,300 human respondents, 5-point purchase-intent Likert; real mean PI ≈ 4.0 ± 0.1 (narrow, noise-dominated — hence the attainment metric).

1. **Direct Likert rating (DLR) collapses.** GPT-4o/Gemini-2.0-flash asked for an integer: KS similarity to human distributions 0.26 (GPT-4o) / 0.39 (Gem-2f) — models reply "3", almost never 1 or 5, while humans mass at 4–5. Prompt-nudging toward extremes raises distributional similarity but *destroys ranking signal* ("over-correction subverts the goal") — the two axes trade off under direct numeric elicitation.
2. **Textual elicitation + SSR fixes both axes.** Free-text statement → embed (text-embedding-3-small) → cosine vs 5 anchors per Likert level → pmf. Result: ρ ≈ 90% of the human test-retest ceiling, KS ≈ 0.80–0.88. The follow-up-Likert variant (a second LLM pass mapping text→integer) improves ranking (ρ 85–90%) but its distributions stay worse (KS 0.59–0.72) — single-integer mapping re-introduces the collapse.
3. **The pmf construction (Eq. 8–9).** p(r) ∝ γ(r) − γ_min + εδ, then optional temperature p^(1/T) renormalized. The γ_min subtraction is load-bearing: within the embedding space the cosine spread between "definitely yes" and "definitely no" anchors is numerically small — without the min-shift the pmf is nearly flat. ε offsets the structural zero (the argmin level always gets 0 at ε=0). Six anchor sets are averaged per response (an ensemble over anchor phrasings; the sets are manually tuned to these 57 surveys — the paper's own limitation).
4. **Demographic conditioning is load-bearing for ranking, not for distribution.** Zero-demography personas produce *better* distributional similarity (KS 0.91 — they just rate everything 4–5 like the human mean) but ρ collapses to ~50%: no ranking signal. Persona conditioning is what makes the model read the concept at all. (The distribution can be right for the wrong reason — the attainment metric is what catches this.)
5. **Attainment beats raw correlation as the success metric.** Real PI is so narrow (σ ≈ 0.1 across concepts) that raw R_xy is bounded by panel noise; ρ = E[R_xy]/E[R_xx] with R_xx from 2,000 split-half retest simulations measures "how much of the achievable signal was recovered." A synthetic panel can be distributionally perfect and ranking-blind; attainment is the axis that separates them (finding 4 is the demonstration).
6. **Beats a trained baseline.** LightGBM trained on half the surveys (demographics + concept features) reaches ρ 65% / KS 0.80 — the zero-shot elicitation beats in-sample-trained supervised ML on ranking, showing the signal lives in semantic reading of the concept text, not the tabular features.
7. **Byproduct:** free-text answers are richer qualitative feedback than human free-text (humans: "It's good"; synthetic personas: specific praises/objections in persona voice).

## 4. Workspace Cousins (signal-diffs — why "not covered")

- **reflex `Lz4FlexDrafter` corpus-is-the-model option scoring:** scores each option from corpus evidence retrieved per query — labels are options, anchors are corpus entries, scores are retrieval-weighted. SSR scores ONE free-text statement against K FIXED ordinal anchor statements. Different input (free text vs question), different anchor semantics (ordinal scale definition vs option evidence). No overlap of mechanism.
- **instinct `corpus_centroid` (signed simhash fold):** one vector per DOMAIN used for routing/admission — a prototype, but at domain granularity for gating, never a per-answer ordinal readout.
- **reflex `readout.rs` confidence:** functionals (entropy/maxprob) OVER an answer distribution we already have; SSR *produces* the distribution from text. Upstream of readout, not a competitor.
- **ceiling-probe (Issue 108, closed negative):** ceiling-normalization cousin; its ceiling is gold-support (does gold actually support an answer, judged κ-gated) and the cell closed on judge-validity. Attainment's ceiling is panel NOISE (split-half self-correlation) — no judge seat needed, computable from labels alone. Different ceiling, different failure surface; the Issue-108 lesson (judge-conditioned ceilings are seat-conditioned) is an argument FOR the noise-ceiling form.
- **PAW lane exact-match law:** the actual tension point. Free-text answers mapped by exact match; unparseable = counted refusal. Deliberate honesty (never a guess). An SSR mapper would map MORE answers — trading refusal-honesty for coverage. The resolution in Issue 132: opt-in, measured, and **degrades to abstain** (§5), never a forced guess.
- **`SigmoidGateCalibrator` + the fused-gate cal-fit law ("thresholds do NOT transfer; fit per suite at cal ρ=30"):** SSR's (ε, T) are the same coefficient class — calibration constants on a projection, and the paper's own limitation says the anchor sets were hand-tuned to these 57 surveys with transfer unknown. Our law predicts exactly that; any in-stack adoption inherits the fit-per-suite discipline.
- **riir-ai G10/G12 human-believability gates (BLOCKED-ON-HUMAN-RATERS):** the only in-fleet ordinal-rating instruments. Considered and REJECTED as consumers (§6).

## 5. The Stack Deltas (what filing Issue 132 actually buys)

**(a) Anchor-pmf + spread-gated abstention (the reflex-native fusion).** SSR's own failure hazard — the flat pmf when the cosine band is narrow (the authors' γ_min motivation) — is a *signal*, not just a nuisance: when (max − min) anchor cosine spread is below a floor, or the winning mass is below a bar, the response is off-manifold and the mapping should ABSTAIN (counted refusal, the PAW law's honesty in latent form) rather than emit a near-uniform pmf. SSR ships ε-smoothing; the stack ships first-class abstention (`CorpusDistanceGate`). This is the one place our architecture is strictly stronger than the paper's, and it is what keeps the exact-match law's refusal-honesty under a semantic mapper. ⚠ Sigmoid-law note: Eq. 8–9 is softmax-family over min-shifted cosines (temperature = softmax τ). The house law (sigmoid, never softmax) binds gating/projections onto learned directions; an ordinal pmf is a distribution output — if implemented, either per-level sigmoid gates + renormalization, or a documented pmf exception in the module doc. Decide at implementation, not here.
**(b) Ordinal arena metrics — with the adaptation bound stated.** The harness measures accuracy/ECE; the paper shows the two-axis evaluation (distribution realism × ranking signal) catches failure modes accuracy hides — the zero-demography persona (finding 4) is the exact shape of a hosted lane that regresses to suite priors and looks fine on accuracy while carrying no signal. The adaptation to our data has a hard structural limit, found in verdict round 1: **the paper's correlation attainment is a BETWEEN-CONCEPT metric (57 concepts with differing true means), and the harness has exactly ONE ordinal suite (sst5 — emotion/ag_news/banking77/etc. carry unordered categorical labels, verified `EMOTION_KEYS`), so a between-suite ρ is UNDEFINED (one point to correlate) and its natural control PASSES BY CONSTRUCTION (a sampler keyed to each suite's gold marginal reproduces every suite mean exactly ⇒ R_xy ≈ R_xx ⇒ attainment high — the control cannot fail, so it is not a control).** What transfers: per-suite KS (cumulative, ordinal suites only — sst5) and a separately-labeled total-variation axis for unordered suites; and the ranking axis goes WITHIN-suite per-case — Spearman between the lane's expected label E[r] = Σ r·p(r) and gold over sst5 cases, where the suite-prior sampler scores ≈ 0 and IS a real control. No ρ ratio is claimed (the split-half retest ceiling needs repeated ratings per item our datasets do not carry; a phrase-level sentiment ceiling exists in the underlying SST corpus only if the harness carries it — check at implementation, else raw Spearman + CI). Small, CI-regenerable, no serving-path change.

**Rulewright check (priority-#1 ladder, honest answer):** no consumer surface. The elicitation-shape rule candidate (`llm-judge-direct-likert-elicits-collapsed-distribution`, suggestion-only measurement-honesty) has a bounded detector (a benchmark harness prompting an LLM judge for an integer on a scale) but **zero firing population in the workspace** — ceiling-probe's judge is binary-support (κ gate), rubric-probe is a modelless identity control, G10 is human raters, PAW maps text (it never asks for the integer). Recorded as mining-queue lane-intel only; filing a rule with no in-tree firing population is the grade-B shape, and the refine batch lane owns its own queue.

## 6. Rejected Consumers (named, with reasons)

- **riir-ai G10/G12 (crowd believability, blocked on human raters):** LLMs cannot watch the clips; caption-then-rate pipelines would rate the captioner's summary, not the behavior, and the paper's own validity condition — training-data alignment with the judged domain — is worst exactly here (game-believability judgments are far from consumer-review corpora). Synthetic raters would also weaken what the gate is FOR (human-perception evidence). Stays blocked on humans.
- **Modelless engine readout:** no free text exists in the modelless path; SSR has no input to consume.
- **ESC think leg / hosted-expert / ext-seat rerank:** single-question pick-or-abstain contracts; no ordinal text mapping anywhere in the chain.
- **Corpus synthesis (riir-train-adjacent):** persona-conditioned SSR could synthesize soft-labeled ordinal training rows, but our synth lane's teachers already return keyed-probs (soft labels native), so SSR adds elicitation cost for a capability already served. Note-only, no plan.
